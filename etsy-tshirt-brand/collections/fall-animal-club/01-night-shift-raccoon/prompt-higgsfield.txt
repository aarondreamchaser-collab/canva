PROMPT A — ILUSTRACIÓN DE ESPALDA (gráfico principal)
═══════════════════════════════════════════

SUBJECT: An anthropomorphic adult raccoon, waist-up, three-quarter view,
leaning one elbow on a stylized street lamp post, free hand holding a
retro metal lunchbox at his side. Serious, determined, slightly
half-lidded "end of night shift" expression — deadpan, mouth closed,
never cute or smiling.

WARDROBE: Unbuttoned work coverall open at the chest revealing a plain
undershirt, sleeves rolled up past the elbow, a sewn name-patch shape on
the chest left blank (no legible text on the patch). A hard hat tilted
back on one ear.

COMPOSITION: Vintage mascot/badge illustration style, framed for a large
back print, generous negative space around the figure, no photographic
background — only the stylized lamp post and a minimal hint of pavement
texture as part of the graphic itself, everything else transparent.

GRAPHIC STYLE: Clean vector-style line illustration, vintage screen-print
mascot look, flat shading with subtle halftone texture in shadow areas
only. No 3D rendering, no photorealism, no soft gradients.

ILLUSTRATION STYLE: 80s/90s American workwear mascot / guild-badge
illustration, bold clean linework, confident silhouette.

LIGHTING: Flat graphic lighting, single implied lateral-top light source
used only to separate planes (helmet / coverall / skin) with one flat
shadow tone — not photorealistic lighting.

TEXTURE: Screen-print texture with fine halftone dot shading restricted to
shadow zones.

COLOR PALETTE: Maximum 3 flat ink colors — rust orange (#B5502A) for the
coverall, cream/off-white (#EFE6D3) for the undershirt and highlights,
faded black (#2B2723) for linework and shadow. No saturated or neon
colors.

PRINT APPEARANCE: Clean 2-3 color screen-print look, crisp defined edges,
no noise or artifacts, optimized for DTG print on a dark faded-black
garment.

DISTRESSING: Subtle fine-grain ink cracking texture on the solid coverall
color areas only, medium-low intensity — illustration must stay 100%
legible at t-shirt scale.

PERSPECTIVE: Subtle low-angle (slight worm's-eye feel), heroic but not
exaggerated comic-book angle.

SCALE / POSITIONING: Designed for a large back placement, roughly
30-33 cm wide at its widest point, 35-38 cm tall.

BACKGROUND: Clean transparent background, no scene, no environment beyond
the described graphic elements.

NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS: no mockup, no t-shirt worn, no model, no
photorealistic rendering, no watermark, no signature, no extra background
scenery, no glasses/coffee-cup raccoon cliché, no gradient soft-shading,
no busy clutter, no cropped limbs.

TEXT: NO TEXT / NO LETTERING — the name-patch shape must be left blank.
Any wording is added later in Canva.

PROMPT B — INSIGNIA DE PECHO (logo pequeño)
═══════════════════════════════════════════

SUBJECT: The same raccoon character, simplified to a circular badge/patch
bust portrait — head and shoulders only, hard hat included — reduced
linework detail compared to the back illustration.

COMPOSITION: Circular vintage patch/badge shape with an engraved-looking
border ring, empty banner space at the bottom of the badge left blank for
text to be added later in Canva.

GRAPHIC STYLE / TEXTURE / LIGHTING / DISTRESSING: Same treatment as
Prompt A — flat vector illustration, screen-print halftone shading,
subtle ink-crack distressing, no photorealism.

COLOR PALETTE: Same 3-ink palette as Prompt A (rust orange, cream, faded
black).

PRINT APPEARANCE: Clean 2-3 color screen-print look, crisp edges,
optimized for small-scale DTG print.

SCALE / POSITIONING: Designed for a small left-chest placement, roughly
8-9 cm wide.

BACKGROUND: Clean transparent background.

NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS: no mockup, no t-shirt worn, no model, no
photorealistic rendering, no watermark, no gradient soft-shading, no
busy clutter.

TEXT: NO TEXT / NO LETTERING — leave the bottom banner shape empty.
